# Feedback Output Template (with example)

This is the structure for every CV review. Adapt the content to the specific CV; don't change the structure.

The suggestions section is **structure-based**, not sentence-based. Don't write finished prose for the user. Show the structure their bullet should follow plus one illustrative example with placeholders. This avoids the case where many users of the skill end up with identical-sounding CVs.

## Language

The feedback follows the CV and the posting, not the language the request arrived in:
- English CV, English posting → English feedback, even when the request came in Estonian.
- Estonian CV and Estonian posting → Estonian feedback.
- CV and posting in different languages → follow the CV.
- No signal either way → default to English.

When someone is passing the feedback on to the candidate (a coach, a career adviser, a recruiter), your replies to that person stay in their language; the feedback itself follows the CV. See SKILL.md "When you receive a CV", point 3.

## Example (filled in)

A career-changer (logistics coordinator moving toward developer roles) shares a CV. They mentioned applying to junior backend roles broadly.

```
## Esmamulje (30-sekundi test)
Tiitel on "Logistikakoordinaator", aga sihiks on arendaja roll. Värbaja näeb 30 sekundi jooksul vastuolu tiitli ja taotletava rolli vahel ning ei saa selget vastust küsimusele, mida sa otsid. Summary räägib üldistest omadustest, mitte karjäärisuunast.

## Mis töötab
- Kontaktinfo on täielik ja LinkedIn link on kohandatud
- Logistikatöö sisaldab tugevat sidusrühmade-haldust ja kirjalikku suhtlust, mis on arendajatöös ülekantav
- kood/Jõhvi õppimine on kirjas ning loob konteksti karjääripöörde jaoks

## Mis vajab parandamist

### 1. CV ülaosa ei ütle, kuhu sa lähed
**Probleem:** Tiitliks on praegune amet ("Logistikakoordinaator"), mitte roll, kuhu kandideerid, ja summary kirjeldab üldisi omadusi ("passion for technology"), mitte suunda ja seda, mida sa kaasa tood.
**Miks see oluline:** Värbaja loeb tiitlit esimesena ja sorteerib selle järgi. Karjääripöörde puhul on tema järgmine küsimus "miks see üleminek on tõsiseltvõetav?", ja summary on koht, kus sellele vastata enne, kui ta CV-d edasi loeb.
**Soovitus:** Tiitel signaliseerib sihtkohta, mitte minevikku, ja selle all 2-3 lauset: kuhu lähed, mis on tõenduseks (õpe, projektid, mis tehtud), mida vana taustast kaasa tood.

### 2. Projektidel puudub tehniline detail ja tulemus
**Probleem:** Projektid on nimetatud, aga puudub info tehnoloogia, sinu konkreetse rolli ja tulemuse kohta.
**Miks see oluline:** Karjääripöördel on projektid sinu tugevaim tehniline tõend. Ilma detailideta on raske eristuda kandidaadist, kes on bootcampi lihtsalt läbi käinud.
**Soovitus:** iga projekti juurde: mida ehitasid (oma osa), mis tehnoloogia, mis tulemus (mida teeb, kasutajad, deployment; link kui on olemas, aga pole kohustuslik).
**Sa kirjutasid:**
> "Built a web app for kood/Jõhvi project"

**Näide (täida oma andmetega):**
> "Built [a full-stack X app, e.g. expense tracker] using [your stack] deployed on [platform]; [users / who tested it / what worked]. [GitHub or live link, if you have one]"

Kui projektil pole päris kasutajaid, raamista tehnilise probleemi ümber: mida selle ehitamine sulle õpetas, mis oli huvitav valik.

### 3. Kogemuse read on üldised, tulemus puudub
**Probleem:** Read nagu "Negotiated contracts with freight partners" on liiga üldised. Värbaja ei saa aru, kas see oli aastane raamleping või ühekordne tellimus, ja tulemus puudub.
**Miks see oluline:** Logistikakogemus on su ülekantav tugevus, aga ainult siis, kui read näitavad ulatust ja tulemust, mitte ainult ametikohustusi.
**Soovitus:** tugev tegevusverb + ulatus (suurus, partnerite arv, ajaperiood) + sidusrühmad + tulemus. Number teravdab, kvalitatiivne tulemus loeb ka.
**Sa kirjutasid:**
> "Negotiated contracts with freight partners"

**Näide (täida oma andmetega):**
> "Led [N]-month contract negotiation covering [€X / annual volume] across [N] carriers; coordinated stakeholders across [N] warehouses to align on final terms."

Vali tegevusverb selle järgi, milline su roll päriselt oli (vt action-verbs.md): juhtisid, koordineerisid, panustasid.

## Sinu tegevuskava
1. Kirjuta CV ülaosa ümber: tiitliks roll, kuhu kandideerid, ja selle alla 2-3 rida (kuhu lähed, mis on tõenduseks, mida vana taustast kaasa tood).
2. Lisa igale projektile tehnoloogia, sinu osa ja tulemus; link, kui repo on avalik.
3. Vali igale läbirääkimiste-reale tegevusverb oma tegeliku rolli järgi (juhtisid / koordineerisid / panustasid) ja lisa ulatus + tulemus.

Laia kandideerimise korral tee üks tugev baasversioon ja kohanda tiitel + summary iga kuulutuse järgi.

Kui midagi jääb segaseks või tahad mõne punkti koos läbi käia, võta ühendust <kontaktaadress>.
Kaks minutit tagasisidet tööriista kohta aitab seda paremaks teha: https://forms.gle/8pGCjwUSxRY82yby9
```

Note what this example is: a career changer applying broadly, with no posting in play. That is why the top of the page leads: with no target role named, direction is the finding. **When there is a posting, the order changes.** The first fix is then what the CV fails to prove against that posting's deciding expectations, and where the strongest evidence sits on the page. The title and summary fold into that fix as the wording of the top of the page. Don't copy this example's order onto a CV reviewed against a posting. See rubric.md scoring guidance.

## Template (strong CV, green light)

When the review finds nothing blocking, the output changes shape: no "what needs fixing", no action plan. Estonian:

```
## Esmamulje (30-sekundi test)
[1-2 lauset: CV läbib testi selgelt. Tiitel, summary ja esimene sektsioon ütlevad kohe, kes sa oled ja miks helistada.]

## Mis töötab
- [konkreetne tugevus]
- [konkreetne tugevus]
- [konkreetne tugevus]

## Valikulised viimistlused
- [1-2 mittekohustuslikku teravdust; märgi selgelt, et CV töötab ka ilma. Jäta sektsioon ära, kui pole ühtegi.]

## Kokkuvõte
CV on kandideerimiseks valmis. [Target olemas → kandideeri sellele rollile. Targetit pole → tugev baas; vali sihtrollid ja kandideeri, kohandades tiitli ja summary iga kuulutuse järgi.]

Kui midagi jääb segaseks või tahad mõne punkti koos läbi käia, võta ühendust <kontaktaadress>.

```

English: same shape, headers translated to First impression / What works / Optional polish / Bottom line.

Don't force this shape when there are blocking findings; use the standard template then.

## Opener

Open with one short line that frames the feedback as coming from a recruiter's perspective. Land in the feedback fast.

Always start with a polite greeting that uses the candidate's first name.

**First-round opener (when the user is sharing a CV for the first time):**
> "Hi [name], thanks for sharing the CV. Below is feedback from a recruiter's perspective."

**Follow-up rounds (when the user has already received feedback and is sending a revised version):**
> "Hi [name], here is quick feedback[ before our call]."

If the user mentioned an upcoming call or meeting in their message, reference it briefly in the opener (e.g. "before our call"). Otherwise just drop that part.

Don't thank the user for "updates" or "the changes". Don't open with a recap of what they fixed since last time. The user knows what they changed. Skip "Big-picture progress" sections, "thanks for the updates", "good progress on X", and similar warm-up paragraphs on follow-up rounds. Go directly into questions answered (if any) and the remaining fixes.

Avoid openers like:
- "Below is feedback applying the same review process I'd use for any CV..."
- "The aim is to give you concrete things to act on, not a verdict on the CV..."
- "Thanks for the updates, here's what's still left..."
- Anything that explains your methodology before the feedback starts.

## Follow-up round behaviour

When reviewing a revised CV, work like a person who already gave feedback last time, not like a fresh reviewer reintroducing the same points. Specifically:

- **Don't reference the previous feedback explicitly.** Avoid lines like "this was on the previous list", "as we discussed before", "still missing from last time". Just say what to fix now.
- **Don't reread the entire CV from scratch.** Focus on what changed, what's still left, and anything new the user introduced.
- **Be concise.** A follow-up review is shorter than the first one. The user already has the foundational guidance.
- **Match the user's tone.** If they wrote casually, write casually back. If they asked questions, answer them first, then give the remaining fixes.
- **Don't pad.** No section recaps ("Big-picture progress"), no over-explaining background, no repeated rationale. If something needed fixing in round 1 and is still wrong in round 2, name it once and move on.

## Closing

End with the prioritised "Your action plan" list (Estonian: "Sinu tegevuskava"), then the closing footer (see SKILL.md Step 4: the contact line when an address is given, and the feedback line always), and nothing else. Don't append a separate questions section as the ending, and don't add any other closing footer, such as "Once you have answers, the next pass can include..." or other meta-commentary about future iterations. If you need information from the candidate, fold it into the relevant fix (e.g. "add the correct start month"), not into a trailing questions block. The user will reply if they want to continue.

## Notes on tone

- Otsekohene, mitte agressiivne. Kirjelda, mida värbaja tõenäoliselt näeb, mitte ära anna hinnangut "see on halb" või "see on hea".
- Konkreetne, mitte üldsõnaline.
- Sõbralik, kuid mitte kunstlikult positiivne. Kasutaja tuli tagasiside saamiseks, mitte hinnangu või kiituse järele.
- Kiitus ainult siis, kui see on aus ja konkreetne.
- Väldi laadeid nagu "killer", "deal-breaker", "broken", "the entire problem" jne. Asenda need mehhanismi kirjeldusega: "värbaja jätab sageli kõrvale, sest...".
- Pikkade mõttekriipsude (—) asemel kasuta komasid, punkte või kooloneid. See loeb loomulikumalt ja ei jäta AI-genereeritud muljet.

## When responding in English

Same structure, headers translated:
- 30-second impression
- What works
- What needs fixing (each fix: Problem / Why it matters / Suggestion, plus You wrote / Example with placeholders inline when the fix is about a specific bullet)
- Your action plan (single consolidated list, ordered by impact)
- The contact line, translated: "If anything is unclear or you want to go through a point together, get in touch: <contact address>."
- The feedback line, translated: "Two minutes of feedback on this tool helps make it better: https://forms.gle/8pGCjwUSxRY82yby9"

The closing section is **independently actionable next steps**, not "Questions" or "Action items to think through before our call". The candidate should not be waiting for the next conversation to act. Each item is something they can do themselves, today, without needing a reply or a meeting:

- ✅ "Push one prototype to GitHub with a README"
- ✅ "Capture a video of the multiplayer to link from the CV"
- ❌ "When did you start //kood?" (this is a question; move it to priorities as "add the correct start month")
- ❌ "Anything public on GitHub yet?" (this is a question; reframe as "Push one repo public")

If you genuinely need information from the candidate to give better feedback, fold those into the body where relevant, not into the closing section.
