# //kood Context

This reference exists so the skill can frame //kood graduates correctly when reviewing their CVs. Many recruiters and hiring managers, especially outside Estonia, will not have heard of //kood. Your job in CV feedback is to help the candidate articulate the value of their training in concrete terms a recruiter immediately understands, not to lean on the school name as a credential.

## Naming

The school is referred to as **//kood**. Specific campuses use the slash form:

- **kood/Jõhvi**
- **kood/Võru**
- **kood/Järva**

Use either **//kood** (the school as a whole) or **kood/Jõhvi** (a specific campus), depending on what the candidate is referring to. **Don't combine them as "//kood/Jõhvi"** — that's incorrect; the `//` prefix and the `/Campus` suffix are alternative naming conventions, not stacked. "Kood" alone (without `//` or campus suffix) is also incomplete; recommend correcting it.

## What //kood is

//kood is an alternative programming school in Estonia. It operates its own curriculum and learning platform.

The model is built around **project-based, hands-on, team-driven learning**:

- **No teachers, no lectures.** Students learn by working through a structured progression of programming projects.
- **Heavy teamwork.** Many projects are completed in teams; collaboration is a core skill the program selects for and develops. Peer review across the cohort is part of how learning happens, but on the CV it's enough to surface the teamwork angle, the peer-review wording is school-internal jargon and adds little for a recruiter.
- **Self-paced.** Each student advances through the curriculum at their own tempo.
- **Hybrid.** Students can learn on campus or remotely.
- **12 to 15 months** total, after a competitive admissions process (logic game test plus a three-week Selection Sprint).

Graduates typically move into roles as software developers, full-stack engineers, DevOps engineers, QA specialists, or product engineers.

## What this means for a CV

The CV should surface what //kood actually trains, not lean on the school name as a credential:

- **Project-based problem-solving experience.** Graduates ship real projects, often production-like in scope. The CV should show projects with the problem solved, tech stack, the candidate's specific role, and a link.
- **Teamwork.** Many //kood projects are completed in teams. This is a concrete differentiator from candidates who learned alone, and it should show up in how the candidate describes their role on group projects.
- **Not the school name.** Don't lean on "kood/Jõhvi" as a credential a recruiter is expected to recognise. The work the candidate did is what carries the CV.

**A short factual line about //kood on the Education entry should be lean.** Recommended phrasing: "Project-based software development program with hands-on teamwork." Keep it under 15 words. Don't list specific languages (Java, etc.) here unless the program is genuinely language-specific for that cohort, since //kood covers many languages across its tracks. Don't write "peer review" on the CV; it's school-internal jargon that doesn't translate.

## Tutoring and tech coordinator work is customer support experience

Many //kood students tutor or work as tech coordinators at a campus. On a CV this almost always reads as mentoring, and for support, service desk, QA or operations roles that framing undersells it. The work is a support desk: a request arrives, the tutor reproduces the problem, then resolves it or escalates it.

The people arriving with the problem are Selection Sprint participants and applicants, and they are often under real pressure. Staying calm and supportive with someone stressed is part of the job, not a side effect of it. That is the same skill a support engineer needs with the users of a business-critical system, and it maps directly onto what postings ask for as "customer support experience", "a can-do attitude when dealing with complex situations", and "a team-player mindset". Treat the tutoring entry as the candidate's customer-facing experience, and say so.

When a //kood student applies to a support, service desk, QA or operations role, recommend rewriting the tutoring entry from the support side: what came in, what they did, what happened after. Volume, reproduction and follow-through are the three things a support team reads a junior on. Don't leave the entry as "mentored N students, reviewed projects".

## Audience awareness

Most //kood candidates apply within Estonia, where many recruiters know the school. Some recruiters, especially outside Estonia, still won't have heard of it. Frame the //kood line on the CV so it works either way: full name (//kood, kood/Jõhvi) plus a short factual description of what the program is (project-based, teamwork). Recruiters who already know the school will recognise it; those who don't will get the context they need.

When giving feedback, don't assume the recruiter doesn't know //kood. Say something like "many recruiters know //kood, but not all do" rather than "most recruiters outside Estonia don't know //kood".

## Details matter

Small inconsistencies in how //kood is written undercut the CV, especially because the school's name is uncommon. Specifically:

- **//kood**, not "Kood" or "kood".
- **kood/Jõhvi** (or kood/Võru, kood/Järva), not "Kood Jõhvi" or "Jõhvi Kood".
- Get this right on the CV. A candidate who can't write their school's name correctly signals lack of attention to detail to a reader who knows the school.

## How to write //kood on a CV

- Use the full campus name (kood/Jõhvi, kood/Võru, kood/Järva).
- List dates as month + year, not just year. Programs run 12 to 15 months, so the dates should reflect that.
- Don't write "Participated in". The school is project-driven; the verb should match. Suggest stronger verbs from `action-verbs.md` (Built, Completed, Delivered).
- Include a short factual note about the program type if there's room: project-based, peer-learning, team projects. This educates the recruiter without hyping.
- Bring objective markers if the candidate has them: completed all core modules, top of cohort, specific project tracks. Don't invent any.
- The strongest value comes from the **projects themselves**, not the school name. The Projects section is what carries //kood graduate CVs.

## What to avoid in feedback about //kood

- **Don't compare //kood to other schools** (École 42, 01 Edu, university CS programs, etc.). //kood has its own curriculum and platform. Any comparison risks being inaccurate or dated, and adds nothing for the recruiter.
- **Don't oversell the school name.** The candidate's CV should not lean on "kood/Jõhvi" as a credential a recruiter is expected to recognise. Lean on the work the candidate did instead.
- **Don't claim guaranteed outcomes** ("//kood graduates always land jobs", etc.). The candidate's CV strength is what gets interviews, not the program label.
